THE BATTLEFIELD

Interview Rounds

Know what to expect and how to clear every stage.

ROUND 01 / APTITUDE
🧮

Quantitative & Logical Reasoning

Companies use aptitude tests to evaluate numerical ability, reasoning, and speed.

  • Percentages and ratios
  • Time, speed and distance
  • Probability
  • Number series
  • Logical reasoning
TIP: PRACTICE DAILY
ROUND 02 / GROUP DISCUSSION
🗣️

Group Discussion

Demonstrate communication, teamwork, confidence, and structured thinking.

  • Understand the topic
  • Start with a clear introduction
  • Give logical arguments
  • Respect others' opinions
  • Summarize effectively
TIP: BE CLEAR, NOT LOUD
ROUND 03 / TECHNICAL
💻

Technical Interview

Show that you understand computer science fundamentals and can explain your work.

  • OOPS concepts
  • DBMS and SQL
  • Operating Systems
  • Computer Networks
  • Programming fundamentals
  • Projects and technologies
TIP: UNDERSTAND, DON'T MEMORIZE
ROUND 04 / CODING
⌨️

Coding Assessment

Demonstrate your ability to write correct, efficient, and readable code.

  • Arrays and strings
  • Sorting and searching
  • Loops and functions
  • Basic recursion
  • DSA problem-solving
TIP: EXPLAIN YOUR APPROACH
ROUND 05 / HR
🤝

HR Interview

Demonstrate your attitude, professionalism, motivation, and communication.

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why should we hire you?
  • Your strengths and weaknesses
  • Why this company?
  • Career goals
TIP: BE AUTHENTIC

STRATEGY & EXECUTION

How to Clear Interviews

Small improvements in preparation can make a big difference.

01. Research the company

Understand its products, services, business model, role requirements and recent developments.

02. Prepare your resume

Be ready to explain every skill, project, internship and achievement mentioned in your resume.

03. Master your projects

Explain the problem, architecture, technology stack, your contribution, challenges and outcomes.

04. Practice mock interviews

Simulate real interview conditions. Practice speaking your answers instead of only reading them.

05. Communicate confidently

If you don't know something, admit it honestly and explain how you would approach learning it.

06. Revise consistently

Use active recall, coding practice and short revision sessions to retain concepts.
